Talk. It types.
Nothing leaves your Mac.

OpenFlo is free, open-source voice dictation for macOS. Hold fn, speak naturally and get clean, punctuated text in any app, in under a second, fully offline.

Install free ★ Star on GitHub

Install in one line

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harshkit/openflo/main/install.sh | bash

Requires macOS 26 or newer on Apple Silicon. Installs the local AI engine, a ~1 GB model and the app.

What it does

Hold fn, speak, releaseText lands in Slack, Gmail, Notion, VS Code or any text box.
Cleans up your speechRemoves ums and stutters, applies "no wait, I mean…" corrections and fixes punctuation.
Under a secondStreaming on-device recognition plus a small local LLM on Metal.
Private by designNo account, no telemetry, no servers. Audio is never saved.
Notes, Dictionary, SnippetsThink out loud, keep names spelled right, expand shortcuts.
12 languagesIncluding Indian, US, UK and Australian English.
