Why OpenFlo

You think at 150 words a minute but type at 40. Your ideas deserve better than a keyboard, and your voice deserves better than someone else's server. OpenFlo listens, understands and writes, entirely on your Mac.

Features

Speak messy.
Send polished.

⚡

Under a second

Speech streams to Apple's on-device recognizer while you talk, then a local AI tidies it up. Text lands ~0.6–1.0 s after you let go.

✨

Understands corrections

"Meet at 5, no wait, 6" becomes "Meet at 6." Ums, stutters and false starts disappear, and spoken lists become numbered lists.

🌐

Every app

Slack, Gmail, Notion, VS Code, ChatGPT, Messages. If it has a text box, OpenFlo types into it.

🗒️

Notes

A scratchpad for thinking out loud. Capture ideas hands-free and copy them anywhere.

📖

Dictionary & snippets

Your names and jargon spelled your way. Say "my calendar link" and get your full URL.

🔉

Whisper-quiet

Automatic gain picks up soft or distant speech, so you can dictate in a quiet office.

Install

One line.
Then just talk.

Terminal
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harshkit/openflo/main/install.sh | bash

Installs the local AI engine, a ~1 GB model and the app. Allow Microphone and Accessibility, set the 🌐 key to "Do Nothing", and hold fn anywhere.
